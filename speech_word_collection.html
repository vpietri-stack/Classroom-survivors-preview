How we will use these recordings

First compare recognition, target-aware decisions and checking time on your clear, quick and contrasting-word takes. Report quiet, mumbled, imitated-accent and non-speech results separately. Homophones are equivalent spellings, not wrong words.

The plan reserves whole word families for a later check. All versions of a family stay together. Your voice is useful development evidence; it cannot establish accuracy for children. The existing student development recordings remain a separate evaluation, and the reserved student test recordings stay unused until the approach is fixed. We can choose different models for words and sentences if the results support it.
